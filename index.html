Overview

TaxiDriver AI is an intelligent co‑pilot for professional drivers. It combines airport arrivals, rail and coach feeds, live traffic, weather and local events to forecast pickup hotspots and guide drivers to the next best fare.

  • Hotspot forecasting: predictive demand maps around airports, stations, venues.
  • Multi‑modal arrivals: flights, trains, coaches unified and ranked by revenue potential.
  • Driver analytics: session earnings, success rate, time‑to‑fare and ROI trends.

See the value Explore the platform

Value

Drivers spend a significant part of each shift searching for fares or waiting in the wrong place. TaxiDriver AI reduces wasted time (dead miles) and increases earnings by navigating drivers to predicted demand.

  • −20–35% dead miles by positioning near incoming arrivals and events.
  • +10–25% earnings/shift from higher fare density and better job mix.
  • Faster time‑to‑fare via live hotspot ranking and queue intelligence.
  • Lower fuel costs through smarter routing and fewer empty journeys.

Figures are model‑based projections; real‑world results vary by city, driver behavior and supply/demand.

Platform

  • Real‑time arrivals: Airports (flights), National Rail, and coach feeds unified into one timeline.
  • Demand scoring: weather impact, event proximity, time‑of‑day and historical patterns.
  • Hotspots: interactive map with dynamic radius filtering and ranked pickup zones.
  • Driver analytics: session tracking, earnings, success rate and performance trends.
  • Notifications: real‑time alerts for high‑value arrivals and shifting demand.

Investors

TaxiDriver AI targets the £100B+ global ride‑hailing and taxi market with a data‑driven decision layer for professional drivers and fleets.

  • Product: Live demand forecasting from multi‑modal arrivals, weather and events.
  • Customer: Independent drivers and mid‑size fleets in airport/urban markets.
  • Business model: Subscription per driver with fleet discounts.
  • Traction (prototype): Completed integrations, full test coverage, Dockerized deployment.
